Here's a simple breakdown of the MVP blueprint:
What it is: A social app where every post is a verified, real human moment — permanently stored and impossible to fake with AI.

5 main components:

Anti-AI Camera — No uploading from your gallery. Photos are taken live in-app, with the phone's gyroscope and sensors checking it's a real 3D environment (not a screen). Every shot is cryptographically sealed with GPS and timestamp the moment it's taken.
Invisible Web3 — Users sign up with Google/email like any normal app. A crypto wallet is secretly created in the background, and the platform covers all transaction fees — no seed phrases, no crypto knowledge needed.
Permanent Storage — Photos go to decentralized storage (Arweave), not regular cloud servers that can be deleted. A blockchain ledger records the proof: who posted it, when, where, and what it shows.
Social + Gamification — Posts are tagged into life categories (Nature, Culture, Community, Milestones). Completing verified actions earns you non-transferable blockchain badges ("Soulbound Tokens") that build your "Humanity Score."
Community Trust — New users need 3 verified humans to vouch for them to reach full "Verified Human" status. Questionable posts can be flagged and voted on by the community.


The simple user flow: Sign up → Take a live photo → App verifies it's real → Saved permanently → You earn a badge → It appears on the global map.
